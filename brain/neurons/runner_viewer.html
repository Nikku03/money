Simplex Runner

An original three-lane endless runner. Walls mean switch lanes, hurdles mean jump, beams mean duck, and the speed keeps rising. The eye cells are tuned to time-to-contact, the way a locust's looming detector is, so they fire the same number of frames before impact at any speed. Coincidence cells turn what the eyes see into a command, and a directed 11-simplex clique carries out each move.

Seeing in time, not distance

WALL cells fire 3 to 12 frames before a wall arrives, and HURDLE and BEAM cells 2 to 5 frames before. Commands take about 2 frames to come out of a clique, so these windows lead the moment of action by those 2 frames, at every speed. CLEAR cells are OFF cells that fire when no wall arrives within 13 frames. Spikes can't signal that something is absent, so the eye reports it.

Deciding by coincidence

GO_R0 fires only when LANE0, WALL0 and CLEAR1 all spike at once. Each input carries 0.055, so any two reach 0.11, under the 0.135 threshold. When both sides are clear, the left-going axons are 1.5 ms longer, so RIGHT's source fires first and its inhibitory cell silences LEFT.
