**Reading.**
- **No average edge.** The typical stock gains nothing on its decision day, and most of the pre-decision run-up is
  given back before the answer.
- **The risk is the tail.** A rejection can cut a small company in half, and options already charge for it: Capricor
  ±69-108%, Savara ±29-38%, Summit ±20-26%.
- **Buying options needs a bigger move than that; selling them risks the tail.**
- **The election matters less.** Drug-price policy affects big pharma over years; these dates move single stocks in a
  day.
