# Equations with fear, greed, catalysts, news and attention

Written Nov 2, 2026. **Exploratory**: no test was declared. The question: does adding human and news information to
the math make predictions better?

## Turning them into numbers

Every input is known at the day's close.

| Kind | Inputs |
|---|---|
| Math | Today's move size; the average size over the last 5 and 22 days |
| Fear & greed (the market's mood, the Fear & Greed index's parts rebuilt from the bank) | VIX level; its 5-day jump; VIX against its 50-day average; SPY against its 125-day average; share of universe stocks above their 50-day average; 52-week highs minus lows; stocks against Treasuries (SPY minus TLT, 20 days); junk bonds against Treasuries (JNK minus IEF, 20 days) |
| Catalysts & news | Earnings tomorrow; earnings first session today; another 8-K today (deals, executives, lawsuits); a Fed decision, CPI report or jobs report tomorrow (`knowledge/macro_calendar.json`) |
| Attention & greed in one stock | Volume against its 60-day average; the month's biggest up-day; distance below the 52-week high |

**Where the dates come from.** Earnings and 8-K dates come from the bank's SEC filings (an earnings date is taken as
known ahead, as companies announce it). Fed, CPI and jobs dates come from federalreserve.gov and the BLS release
archives.

**The equations.** Each version is a least-squares equation for the log of tomorrow's move size. Its misses over
the fit years give the 10/25/50/75/90% range. The "spread equation" is a second equation for how wide that range
should be.

**Protocol.**

| Step | Period |
|---|---|
| Fit | 2016 to Jun 2026 (stocks: a 20% sample of stock-days) |
| Tune: compare versions, improve | Jul-Sep 2026 |
| Test, run once | October 2026 |

The score is the pinball loss of the range; lower is better.

## Single stocks: tomorrow's move size

Every research-universe stock: 99,924 stock-days in the tune months, 34,370 in October. "Vs A" is the improvement
over the chain.

| Version | Tune | Vs A | October | Vs A | 80% range held (Oct) | Earnings days (Oct) | Other days (Oct) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: chain (today's move only) | 0.4374 | | 0.4500 | | 79.9% | 1.732 | 0.4190 |
| B: math (+ 5/22-day average) | 0.4062 | 7.1% | 0.4230 | 6.0% | 79.3% | 1.762 | 0.3906 |
| C: + fear & greed | 0.4071 | 6.9% | 0.4233 | 5.9% | 79.2% | 1.758 | 0.3910 |
| D: + catalysts & news | 0.3974 | 9.1% | 0.4136 | 8.1% | 79.3% | 1.319 | 0.3917 |
| E: + attention | 0.3965 | 9.4% | 0.4102 | 8.8% | 79.4% | 1.301 | 0.3886 |
| G: + each stock's earnings habit | 0.3950 | 9.7% | 0.4086 | 9.2% | 79.4% | 1.237 | 0.3886 |
| H: G without fear & greed | 0.3941 | 9.9% | 0.4078 | 9.4% | 79.6% | 1.233 | 0.3879 |
| **H2: H + spread equation (chosen on tune)** | **0.3932** | **10.1%** | **0.4069** | **9.6%** | 79.5% | **1.222** | **0.3871** |

- **Math was the biggest single step.** A stock's recent average move beats yesterday's move alone.
- **Catalysts and news helped a lot on earnings days.** The earnings-day score improved 25%, and 30% with each
  stock's own earnings habit (its median first-day earnings move, eight reports).
- **The weights read like behaviour:**
  - the day before earnings expects a move about 8 times a normal day's;
  - a volume surge today means a bigger move tomorrow (+10% per doubling);
  - a stock far below its 52-week high moves more;
  - jobs-report days move 4% more, Fed days 11% more, CPI days 2% more.
- **The market's fear and greed added nothing for single stocks.** Each stock's own recent moves already carry the
  market's mood. Version H, without them, edged out G.
- The ranges held their odds (79-81% for an 80% range).

## The whole market (SPY): tomorrow's move size, and the money test against the VIX

Each day the VIX prices SPY's next move: an expected one-day move of 0.798 x VIX / sqrt(252). The rule is to sell
when an equation expects 20% less and buy when 20% more. "Gain" is the points of SPY's move by which each bet was
right (price minus move for a sale), before costs.

| Version | Tune range score | Tune bets right | Tune avg gain | October range score | October bets right | October avg gain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: chain | 0.1199 | 11 / 17 | +0.146 | 0.1174 | 7 / 10 | +0.165 |
| B: math | 0.1165 | 36 / 44 | +0.250 | 0.1119 | 18 / 22 | +0.265 |
| C: + fear & greed | **0.1093** | 46 / 56 | **+0.292** | 0.1104 | 15 / 18 | +0.257 |
| D: + Fed / CPI / jobs days | 0.1104 | 44 / 55 | +0.272 | **0.1097** | 16 / 19 | **+0.287** |
| Always sell | | 51 / 64 | +0.266 | | 18 / 22 | +0.265 |

- **For the whole market, fear and greed do help the size forecast.** The range score was 6% better than math in
  the tune months and 2% better in October.
- **The weights:**
  - the VIX itself leads;
  - when stocks beat Treasuries and the market sits above its 125-day average (greed), the next day is calmer;
  - jobs-report days move about 30% more.
- **The money test leans the right way but proves nothing.** C and D were right on 82-84% of their bets, against
  80-82% for always selling, and gained slightly more per bet. These are 64 and 22 days; one bad day undoes that.

## Direction: do fear, greed and catalysts say which way?

These are logistic equations for "up tomorrow" (SPY: also "up over the next five days"). The table gives the AUC:
0.5 is a coin, and Brier scores are against the plain up-day rate.

| Target | Inputs | Tune AUC | October AUC | October Brier vs base rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SPY up tomorrow | Fear & greed | 0.417 | 0.500 | 0.2488 vs 0.2479 |
| SPY up tomorrow | + Fed / CPI / jobs | 0.481 | 0.425 | 0.2526 vs 0.2479 |
| SPY up over 5 days | Fear & greed | 0.519 | 0.597 | 0.2767 vs 0.2377 |
| A stock up tomorrow | Fear & greed | 0.481 | 0.546 | 0.2499 vs 0.2494 |
| A stock up tomorrow | + catalysts, attention, size | 0.489 | 0.538 | 0.2494 vs 0.2494 |

None beat the base rate. The tune months sat at or below a coin flip, and October's AUCs above 0.5 came with Brier
scores no better than the plain rate.

## What it means

1. **Human and news information makes the size forecasts better.** Catalysts, attention and each stock's habits do
   it for single stocks; fear and greed do it for the whole market.
2. **It does not reveal direction.** Fear, greed and news are reflected in prices too fast to call tomorrow's sign
   from them.
3. **The strategy stays a size strategy.** The improved size forecast (H2 for stocks, D for the market) is a better
   input for comparing with what options charge.

Before any of it is trusted, it needs a declared test on days not yet seen (from November 2026), like M1a.
