# The mosaic: what was looked at before its tests were declared

Written Nov 14, 2026. **Exploratory**, 2011-2023 only: nothing here read a filing or a price after 2023-12-31. The
declared tests on 2024-2026 are P1a-P3b in `RESULTS.md` (Mosaic); the engine is `brain/mosaic.py`, described in
`docs/MOSAIC.md`.

The question: people inside a company know before the public that it will sell shares, that its accounts will be
late, that it will cut back, or how its quarter went. Each becomes public later through a dated filing. Do the
public pieces already on file say which companies it will be?

## Data

- **Universe:** the 2,537 stocks of the scan (`universe/us_broad.json`, `us_small.json`, `us_micro.json`,
  `us_watch.json`), all listed today. Companies that failed or were bought are missing.
- **Filings:** each company's full list from SEC EDGAR, with every 8-K's items.
- **Accounts:** SEC XBRL for 2,514 of them, each number dated by the filing that first reported it.
- **Prospectuses:** the first page of all 12,783 prospectuses of the sale forms (424B5, 424B4, 424B1) since 2008,
  read to see what each sells.

| What the prospectus sells | Count |
|---|---|
| Common shares, by the company | 6,714 |
| Bonds or notes | 4,878 |
| Preferred shares | 651 |
| Shares, by holders (a resale) | 525 |
| Not readable from the first page | 15 |

Almost half sell something other than the company's own shares, so counting every such prospectus as a share
sale would have been wrong. Utilities, banks and property companies file these forms for bonds all the time.

- By runway among companies burning cash: under 2 quarters 4.15%, 2-4 quarters 5.26%, 4-8 quarters 4.50%, 8-16

- Other pieces, one at a time: a drug or biotech company 3.77% (others 0.75%); a registration filed in the last 60
  sessions 4.31%; a sale in the last three years 4.34% (none: 0.26%); a net loss 2.47% (a profit: 0.60%).
- **What followed for the 2,934 rows of the fourth line:** over the next 20 sessions their median return was 5.6
  points below SPY's, and their mean 3.6 points above it. The typical one fell behind; a few large winners lifted
  the average. A short position would have lost on average.

## Fitted on 2011-2019, read on 2020-2023

Before declaring anything, each picture's model and its habit-only baseline were fitted on 2011-2019 and read on
2020-2023, at 44 periods 20 sessions apart (97,177 rows). Both spans are inside the final fit's years.

| Picture | Events | Rate | AUC, model / habit | Top 5% by the model had the event | Top 5% held, model / habit | Where the habit is silent: top 5% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A sale of new shares | 1,179 | 1.21% | 0.859 / 0.840 | 8.9% | 37% / 33% | 1.77% against 0.30% |
| Late or unreliable accounts | 428 | 0.44% | 0.848 / 0.796 | 4.5% | 50% / 49% | 1.02% against 0.21% |
| A restructuring or a write-off | 420 | 0.43% | 0.779 / 0.687 | 2.6% | 30% / 30% | 0.97% against 0.28% |

- The habit is most of it. A company with a late-filing notice in the last year files another within 20 sessions
  6.5% of the time; one without, 0.19%.
- The other pieces add a ranking beyond the habit, and a 4 to 6 times lift among companies with no record of the
  event.

**Did the flagged stocks lag?** Each period's top twentieth, against unflagged stocks of the same volatility.

| Picture | 2011-2019 (in the fit): rank against peers | Return against peers | 2020-2023: rank against peers | Return against peers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A sale of new shares | -1.8 points (p 0.003) | -0.66% | -2.1 points (p 0.03) | -0.77% (p 0.29) |
| Late or unreliable accounts | -2.2 points (p 0.0001) | +1.04% | -1.0 point (p 0.04) | +7.19% |

## The coming quarter: looked at, not declared

Two ideas for reading a company's quarter before it reports.

**Its partners already reported.** For each of 73,497 results reports, the stock's eight closest partners (its
market-adjusted daily returns over the prior 250 sessions, correlation 0.30 or more, `brain/connect.py`) were read
at the month-end before. A partner counts when it reported 3 to 45 sessions earlier. The reading is the partners'
average move over the three sessions around their own reports, weighted by correlation. 49,639 reports of 2,225
stocks had one.

| Partners' reading | Reports | The stock rose on its own report | Mean move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positive | 25,876 | 51.8% | +0.45% |
| Negative | 23,763 | 50.6% | +0.16% |
| The tenth most positive | 4,965 | 51.4% | +0.63% |
| The tenth most negative | 4,964 | 50.4% | +0.14% |

- Rank correlation between the reading and the stock's own move: +0.015.
- Long the positive, short the negative: +0.25% a report, t 1.76 over 150 months; t 1.50 in 2011-2017 and 0.94 in
  2018-2023.
- The typical report moved its stock 4.0% either way, so the reading explains almost none of it. By the time a
  company reports, its partners' news is in its price.

**Its report is late.** A report's delay is measured against the same report a year earlier.

| Delay | Reports | The stock rose | Mean move |
|---|---|---|---|
| On time (within 2 sessions) | 43,611 | 50.8% | +0.28% |
| 3-5 sessions late | 7,812 | 50.7% | +0.35% |
| 6-10 sessions late | 1,677 | 47.0% | -0.25% |
| 11+ sessions late | 684 | 51.2% | +0.55% |

One bucket of four leans down and the next leans up. That is not a rule.

Neither was declared as a test. The card (`python -m brain mosaic show`) still lists each partner's latest results.

## Biases

- **Survivorship:** every company is listed today. The ones that sold shares and then failed are missing, which
  flatters the flagged stocks' returns; the ones bought are missing too.
- **Many looks:** three pictures, about 40 pieces and two side studies. The tables above are descriptions; the
  declared tests are the evidence.
- **Today's industry codes** are read back into the past.
- **The first page only:** a prospectus that sells both shares and notes is filed by whichever its first page
  names first.
